Space Weather · Ionospheric Physics

Time–Frequency Characterization of Prompt Penetration Electric Fields Across Distinct Geomagnetic Regimes

S. Rijal · P. Chaudhary · S. Sharma · M. Thapa Thada Magar · B. Regmi · B. Adhikari · et al. — Lead Author

Prompt penetration electric fields carry solar-wind driving directly into the equatorial ionosphere. This study compares their time–frequency behaviour across four geomagnetic conditions — an extreme May 2024 superstorm, a severe April 2023 storm, an October 2017 HILDCAA interval, and a strictly-defined quiet reference day — mapping longitudinal East–West responses across four conjugate meridian pairs.

Condensed Matter · Spintronics

Impurity-Induced Near-Metallicity, Spin-Polarized Transport, and Enhanced Infrared Optical Activity in Mn-Doped Silicon: A Spin-Polarized First-Principles Study

S. Rijal · V. K. Jha · et al.

First-principles calculations show manganese doping collapses silicon's bandgap through strong hybridization between Mn-3d and Si-3p states, pushing the material toward a near-metallic, spin-polarized regime. Screened hybrid (HSE06) calculations confirm the effect isn't a GGA-PBE artifact, and the doped system carries a ferromagnetic ground state with over 57% spin polarization at the Fermi level and roughly a 15-fold rise in thermoelectric power factor between 300 K and 1000 K.

2D Materials · vdW Heterostructures

Strain-Tunable Band Alignment, Optical Anisotropy, and Thermoelectric Response of a Janus MoSSe/Ti₂CO₂ van der Waals Heterostructure

S. Rijal — Sole Author

Stacking a Janus MoSSe monolayer on Ti₂CO₂ MXene breaks inversion symmetry and opens a route to tuning electronic and optical behaviour mechanically rather than chemically. This first-principles study tracks how biaxial strain shifts band alignment across type-I and type-II regimes, reshapes in-plane optical anisotropy, and drives measurable swings in the heterostructure's thermoelectric response — pointing to a single tunable platform for optoelectronic and energy-harvesting applications.
